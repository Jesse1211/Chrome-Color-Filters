## 2. NEW STUDY FOUND — Myers, Drees & Cain 2022 (open access, VERIFIED)
- Citation: Myers E, Drees ET, Cain J. "An Intervention Utilizing the Salience Principle
  to Reduce Pharmacy Students' Psychological Attraction to Smartphones."
  Am J Pharm Educ. 2022;86(4):8717. doi:10.5688/ajpe8717. PMC10159421.
- Design: opt-in challenge, 3rd-year pharmacy students, 2019 + 2020 spring semesters.
  181/274 participated (66%); 173 analyzed. NON-randomized, NO control group, ~3 weeks.
- Intervention was a BUNDLE, not grayscale alone: grayscale + disable social notifications
  + remove social icons from home screen + don't sleep near phone.
- Results: QUALITATIVE ONLY (thematic analysis of open-ended responses). No effect sizes,
  no inferential statistics. Frequencies: improved sleep 20%; "grayscale reduced appeal"
  frequently mentioned; 11% increased awareness; 20% intent to continue; 3% no change;
  **6% compensated by using OTHER DEVICES**; >10% worried about missing communications.
- Stated limitations: opt-in sampling bias, 3-week duration, single open-ended question,
  social desirability bias.
- HOW TO USE: This is CORROBORATING QUALITATIVE evidence, NOT a third quantitative study.
  Because the intervention is confounded (4 changes at once), it CANNOT isolate grayscale.
  Its real value for the explainer: (a) independent report that grayscale "reduced appeal"
  = direct support for the SALIENCE mechanism, in participants' own words; (b) the 6%
  device-substitution finding is an honest limitation worth showing.

## 4. Net effect on the evidence base
Quantitative base remains TWO small short studies (Holte&Ferraro 2020 via secondary;
Dekker & Baumgartner 2024 verified). Myers 2022 adds independent qualitative support for
the salience mechanism specifically. Still: no RCT, no novelty-matched sham, max 2-3 weeks,
durability unknown. Two independent "leakage" findings now exist (Dekker: unlocks unchanged;
Myers: 6% switched devices) plus Holte's video-time null — three separate signals that
grayscale narrows WHERE it works rather than eliminating use.
